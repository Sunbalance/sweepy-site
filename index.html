Common questions

Where do deleted photos go? To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. You can recover them there for 30 days.

Do my photos get uploaded? No. Sweepy works entirely on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I restore Sweepy Pro on a new iPhone? Open the You tab and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sweepy.

Why are some videos marked iCloud? Their full original is only in iCloud, so deleting them frees iCloud space but not space on this iPhone.
